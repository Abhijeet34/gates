# Study: the disk-space alert after raising its threshold

## Recommendation

Keep the disk-space alert at its new 90% threshold.
Alert precision rose from 22.5% to 90%, so most pages now need action.
Alerts fell by 62.5%, from 40 to 15 a month.
No disk filled up unseen in the month after, so the higher threshold missed nothing.

## Evidence

- Month before the change: 40 alerts, 9 of which needed action.
- Month after the change: 15 alerts, 9 of which needed action.
- Disks that reached 100% in the month after: 0.
